Technical Memorandum: Superconducting Transmon Qubit Coherence Metrics
Date: October 2026
Author: Quantum Hardware Group

1. Executive Summary
Recent fabrication advances in tantalum-based superconducting transmon architectures demonstrate marked suppression of dielectric loss. Relaxation time T1 values have been systematically measured exceeding 300 microseconds in multi-qubit planar configurations.

2. Dephasing and Ramsey Coherence
Pure dephasing times (T_phi) show strong resilience against low-frequency flux noise when transmons are biased at the flux-insensitive sweet spot. Ramsey coherence times T2* consistently exceed 180 microseconds across 64-qubit test arrays.

3. Fault-Tolerant Threshold Implications
The observed two-qubit gate fidelities of 99.85% surpass the theoretical fault-tolerance threshold required for surface code quantum error correction. The remaining dominant error mechanism stems from cosmic ray burst events inducing quasiparticle poisoning across the silicon substrate.
